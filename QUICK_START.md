## 📊 Sleep Tracker Enhancements

### NEW Features:

#### 1. Edit Sleep Logs ✏️
- Click **"Edit"** button on any log
- Modify bed time, wake time, or quality
- Click **"Save"** to keep changes
- Click **"Cancel"** to discard

#### 2. Delete Sleep Logs 🗑️
- Click **"Delete"** button (red)
- Log removed immediately
- All changes saved to localStorage

#### 3. Popup Messages 💬

**Low Quality (1-3 stars):**
> "Then you have come to the right place! Get comfy in your bed and turn off the lights. Nyx works best in darkness and a comfy environment."

**High Quality (4-5 stars):**
> "Glad to hear that! :)"

**Popup Behavior:**
- Appears at top center
- Auto-dismisses after exactly **4 seconds**
- Smooth slide-in animation

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Audio Not Playing?
- Check browser allows audio playback
- Click anywhere on page first (browser restriction)
- Check volume isn't muted

### Edits Not Saving?
- Check localStorage is enabled in browser
- Clear cache and reload
- Check browser console for errors

### Popup Not Appearing?
- Make sure you filled in both bed time and wake time
- Check you clicked "Add Sleep Log" button
- Popup appears at very top of page
